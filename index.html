Neighbor P.M.

Machinery sharing and neighborhood assistance

This is a kind of **"Uber for farmers"** – a universal platform for neighborhood assistance and agricultural machinery sharing. The app allows users to lease and share implements or tractors, as well as quickly find seasonal workers. In case of an emergency (SOS), the system ensures instant neighbor notification within a specified radius, allowing the community to react swiftly and provide aid.

Coffefy

New connections and cozy chats over coffee

A **mini "Tinder"** designed to discover new friends and like-minded people for a cozy cup of coffee. Using an intuitive radar, users can view profiles of nearby people and invite them to meet.
